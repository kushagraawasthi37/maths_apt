Super TET Maths Formula Book

How to use this. Pick a topic, read the formula, try both examples before opening the solution, then answer the quick check. Your quiz answers are remembered in this browser.

About the questions. Each formula has a simple example, an exam-pattern example and a quick check, set in the style of the UP Super TET paper: four numbered options, (1) to (4), one correct. Example 1 is a simple direct example; Example 2 and the quick check are in exam pattern. Confirm the syllabus in your official notification.

Tip: write the formula first, then substitute. Convert units before calculating. If a question takes more than about 90 seconds on the first pass, skip it and come back.
